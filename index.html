yt-kesif

A personal, single-user tool that builds YouTube discovery playlists for its owner.

Privacy policy

This app is used only by its developer on their own YouTube account. It reads the owner's subscriptions, liked videos and playlists, and writes only to playlists the app itself created. No data is collected from, shared with, or sold to anyone else. Data stays in the owner's private storage.
